READ WITH CINDERELLA: putting it on the iPad
============================================

This "docs" folder is the whole app. It needs to live on a web address once,
then the iPad keeps its own copy and works offline.

1. PUT IT ONLINE (free, no command line needed)
   - On github.com, create a public repository named read-with-cinderella.
   - Add file > Upload files, drag in the docs and source folders and
     README.txt from the "Reading with Cinderella" folder, Commit changes.
   - Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch; Branch: main, /docs.
   - The app is then at https://jordantong.github.io/read-with-cinderella/

2. INSTALL ON THE iPad
   - Open that address in Safari on her iPad.
   - Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
   - Open Cinderella from the new icon. It runs full-screen and works
     without internet after the first open.

3. FIRST TIME, AS A GROWN-UP (about 10 minutes)
   - Tap "Grown-ups" and answer the multiplication problem.
   - Letter sounds tab: tap "Record them all, one by one" and record the
     28 sounds. Allow the microphone when the iPad asks.
   - Settings: enter her name and tap "Test the voice".

HOW IT SAVES
   - In a browser tab it's a try-it-out preview: nothing is saved.
   - Added to the Home Screen, that icon keeps her gems, island, progress
     and your recordings on that iPad.
   - Save a backup now and then (Grown-ups > Settings > Save a backup).
     Backups include your recordings. Deleting the icon deletes everything
     unless you have a backup.
   - Updates load in place and never erase her progress.
