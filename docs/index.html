Record the letter sounds

  • Just the sound, no “uh.” Say /t/ as a short puff, not “tuh.” The extra “uh” is what makes c‑a‑t sound like “kuh‑ah‑tuh.”
  • Stretchy sounds (m, n, s, f, l, r, v, z, sh, th) can be held for about a second: “mmm.”
  • Bouncy sounds (b, c, d, g, j, k, p, t, ch) are short and a little whispery.
  • Vowels are the short sounds: a as in apple, e as in egg, i as in itch, o as in octopus, u as in up.
  • Each recording is 1.5 seconds, starting the moment you tap. Silence at the start and end is trimmed automatically. Hold the iPad about a foot away in a quiet room.

Backup

Her gems, island, progress and your recorded sounds are saved inside this home-screen app. Updates never erase them. They are lost only if the icon is deleted and added again, or the iPad is reset. Before ever deleting the icon, save a backup here (to Files or iCloud Drive) and restore it afterwards. Backups include your recordings.

Gems and the Shop

She earns 2 gems for a first-try answer and 1 for a second try, plus 3 for the daily treasure and a bonus gem for streaks. Gems buy decorations (25–150), things to wear (25–120), ground colors (20–40), and new lands: Cloud Castle (250), Crystal Cave (450) and Starlight Lake (700).

For a reward from you, or to hand back gems after a reinstall.

Privacy

  • Everything the app knows (her name, progress, gems, island and your voice recordings) is stored on this iPad only. There's no account, no sign-in, and nothing is uploaded.
  • The microphone is used only on the Letter sounds tab, only while you're recording, and the recordings stay on this iPad (and in any backup you save).
  • No cookies, no analytics, no third-party services. Fonts and pictures are built in, so it makes no outside requests once it's open.
  • The talking voice comes from this iPad's own speech settings.
  • Added to the Home Screen, the app works offline. The host it came from sees a normal web request when the app is first downloaded or later updated.
